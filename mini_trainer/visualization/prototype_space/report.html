Questions this opens up

With prototype weights alone

Compare local crowding across taxonomy; follow cross-branch nearest neighbours; measure how many baseline distances tie; examine neighbour stability across checkpoints. Keep alternate metrics alongside the reference rather than silently replacing it.

With held-out image embeddings

Add sample-to-prototype alignment, decision margins, observed occupancy and actual confusion. Those quantities are not inferred from this weight-only report. A sampled decision-territory experiment would be a separate geometric reference.

Provenance and limits
Class and parent IDs come from checkpoint metadata; no online taxonomic remapping. A single checkpoint does not establish a training trajectory. Synthetic cases are deliberately constructed display/calculation probes, not targets. Their smaller class counts do not provide a matched packing comparison. Zero distance may result from CDF rounding and does not prove identical vectors. Grey matrix cells contain no off-diagonal pairs; display flooring never alters stored numerical values.
